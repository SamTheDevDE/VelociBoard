# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public build. VelociBoard is written in Kotlin and runs sidebars on Velocity. It includes per-server boards, priorities, conditions, animations, native and LuckPerms placeholders, cached refreshes, player toggles, commands, and an optional Paper/Folia bridge.

VelocityScoreboardAPI is required on the proxy. Sidebar lines need a Minecraft 1.20.3 or newer client. The Paper bridge currently sends world and block coordinates plus plugin-registered values.
